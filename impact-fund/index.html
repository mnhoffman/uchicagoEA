Programs

The Impact Fund.

A student-run microgrant program funding small, efficient, high-impact charities around the world.

This year, we're excited to pilot the UChicago EA Impact Fund: a microgrant program that provides funding to high-impact charities worldwide that operate efficiently but are too small for the major EA charity evaluators to analyze and fund.

What members do

  • Research and evaluate promising charitable projects around the world

  • Communicate directly with charity organizers and community leaders

  • Gain experience in grantmaking, strategic evaluation, and charity management

  • Track impact and analyze effective charity in practice
