Reading Groups.

Keep learning and discussing after the Intro Fellowship.

Reading groups are a great way to keep learning and discussing after the Intro Fellowship. Dig deeper into the philosophical underpinnings of EA, research the current state of a cause area that's sparked your interest, or choose your own adventure!

Please also use this form to let us know about any projects or ideas you want to implement, or are already working on. We would love to support you, whether by helping you form a group, book rooms, get funding, or connecting you with the broader network of students and professionals in EA.
