About us

Effective altruism at UChicago.

UChicago EA is a group of undergrads, grads, and professional students dedicated to using reason and evidence to tackle the world’s most pressing problems. Through reading and discussion meetups, skill-building workshops, socials, career coaching, and more, we support each other in trying to help others as effectively as we can.
