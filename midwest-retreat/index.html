The Midwest EA Retreat.

A weekend away with students and professionals from across the Midwest. Fall applications are open now.

Apply now (due November 1)

When

Fri to Sun, Nov 13 to 15

Where

DeMotte, Indiana

Cost

Everything covered

Getting there

Rides from campus

Applications are open for the Fall 2026 Midwest EA Retreat: a weekend with around 50 students from EA groups across the Midwest and professionals working in cause areas like AI safety, animal welfare, and global health.

The retreat runs from Friday afternoon through Sunday, November 13 to 15, in DeMotte, Indiana, about an hour from Chicago. Food, accommodation, and travel are all covered, and we arrange rides from campus.

What to expect

  • Talks and Q&As with professionals across cause areas

  • Skill-building workshops and small-group discussions

  • Plenty of unstructured time to meet people from other schools

We encourage anyone who has completed the Intro Fellowship, or has similar familiarity with EA, to apply. Applications are due November 1. Questions? Email Alice Cai at alicecai@uchicago.edu.
