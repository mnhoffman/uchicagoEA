Some of our best memories happen off campus.

We run it

Midwest EA Retreat

Every year, we help organize the Midwest EA Retreat, a weekend that brings together students from EA groups across the Midwest for workshops, talks, games, and the kind of late-night conversations that turn "interested in these ideas" into "part of this community." It's consistently the highlight of the year, and Intro Fellowship participants are eligible to attend.

Dates and sign-up details for the next retreat are coming soon. Join our email listserv to hear first.

We go together

EA Global & EAGx conferences

EA Global conferences gather thousands of students, researchers, and professionals working on the world's most pressing problems, and we attend as a group. It's a chance to meet people doing the work you're curious about, explore career paths, and travel with friends. Travel support is often available for students, and we're happy to help you apply.
