Compassion, melded with rigor.

Doing good can be hard. With so many problems facing the world, from climate change and global poverty to nuclear war and pandemics, it's hard to know which issues matter most, and equally hard to know how best to tackle them.

Effective altruism is a set of ideas premised on a desire to improve the lives of others, and defined by a commitment to reason and evidence. It's also a community: people who help one another use their careers, donations, and resources to do as much good as they can.
